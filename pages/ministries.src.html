Next Generation

Children & Youth Ministry

Nurturing children in the knowledge that they are loved and cherished by God.

We provide Sunday School lessons and family activities where kids learn Bible stories, memorize scripture, and make lasting friendships in a safe, caring church home.

Silver Valley Community Outreach

Serving the physical and spiritual needs of Kellogg and surrounding Shoshone County.

Faith in action is central to our Lutheran heritage. We partner with local food pantries and community resources to support Silver Valley neighbors facing hardship.
