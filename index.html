You Belong Here

Rooted in Scripture, Radiating Christ’s Love

American Lutheran Church is a family of faith rooted in the historic Silver Valley of Northern Idaho.

We believe that God's grace is a free gift for all people. Led by Pastor Craig Shorey, our Sunday gatherings center on traditional Lutheran worship, historic hymnody, biblical preaching, and warm Christian fellowship. Whether you are a lifelong believer or exploring faith, you are always welcome here.
