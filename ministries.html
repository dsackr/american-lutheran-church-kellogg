Life Together

Our Ministries

Growing in faith together, strengthening families, and putting Christ's love into action across Kellogg and Northern Idaho.

Traditional Worship

Worship & Music Ministry

Praising God with joyful voices, sacred liturgy, and timeless Lutheran hymns.

Our worship service is dedicated to the rich heritage of Lutheran liturgical worship, classic hymnody, organ/piano accompaniment, responsive prayers, and scripture readings.

  • Congregational Hymnody & Liturgy
  • Special Music & Choir Anthems
  • YouTube Livestream & Audio Ministry
Next Generation

Children & Youth Ministry

Nurturing children in the knowledge that they are loved and cherished by God.

We provide Sunday School lessons and family activities where kids learn Bible stories, memorize scripture, and make lasting friendships in a safe, caring church home.

Hands of Christ

Silver Valley Community Outreach

Serving the physical and spiritual needs of Kellogg and surrounding Shoshone County.

Faith in action is central to our Lutheran heritage. We partner with local food pantries and community resources to support Silver Valley neighbors facing hardship.

Support Our Outreach Efforts
Adult Discipleship

Small Groups & Fellowship

Connect deeper with fellow Christians through weekly studies and fellowship.

Bible Study

Weekly Bible Study

Engage with Scripture verse-by-verse in an open discussion format. Ask questions, discover context, and apply biblical wisdom to modern everyday life.

Women's Ministry

Women's Circles & Quilting

Enjoy Christian sisterhood, prayer support, and mission projects such as quilt-making for local crisis shelters and Lutheran World Relief.

Fellowship

Sunday Coffee Hour

Join us immediately after the 10:00 AM Sunday worship service in the fellowship hall for hot coffee, baked treats, and uplifting conversation.
