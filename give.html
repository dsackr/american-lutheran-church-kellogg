Your Impact

Where Your Support Goes

Every gift given to American Lutheran Church is handled with strict accountability, transparency, and prayerful stewardship.

  • Preaching the Gospel: Supporting weekly worship, liturgy, music ministry, and our online livestream reaching shut-ins and travelers.
  • Nurturing Youth: Equipping Sunday School teachers with craft supplies, bibles, and family activities.
  • Silver Valley Compassion: Providing emergency grocery cards, food pantry donations, and assistance for Kellogg families in crisis.
