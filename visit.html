Step by Step

What to Expect on Sunday

1

Warm Welcome & Easy Parking

Park in our designated church spots or along Mullan Avenue. When you enter, friendly greeters will hand you a bulletin and answer any questions.

2

Traditional Lutheran Liturgy & Hymns

Services last approximately 60 minutes. We join together in historic Lutheran liturgy, classic hymns, responsive prayers, Scripture proclamation, and a faithful sermon.

3

Open Communion

We celebrate Holy Communion every Sunday. All baptized Christians who believe Jesus is present in the bread and wine are invited to the table.

4

Coffee & Conversation

Stick around after service in the fellowship hall for fresh coffee, tea, and baked goods. It’s the easiest way to make friends in the Kellogg community!

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

Come comfortable! You will see everything from jeans and flannels to suits and dresses. In Kellogg and the Silver Valley, mountain-casual is the norm.

What about my children?

Children of all ages are welcome in the worship sanctuary! We have a children's message and Sunday School activities tailored just for them during the service.

Am I expected to give in the offering?

As our guest, please do not feel obligated to give. The offering is an opportunity for regular attenders to support our ministry and outreach. Your presence is gift enough!

Is the facility wheelchair accessible?

Yes! Our main entrance and sanctuary feature accessible ramp entries and seating areas to ensure comfort for everyone.
